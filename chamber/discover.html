Discover Barranquilla

Manos de Quilla is based in Barranquilla, the capital of the Atlántico department on Colombia's Caribbean coast, where the Magdalena River meets the sea. It's home to roughly 1.2 million residents in the city proper — around 2.3 million across the metropolitan area — and to the country's most celebrated Carnival tradition.
